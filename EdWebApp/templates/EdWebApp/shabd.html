The Beginning...

In 2006, Gautam Ramadurai, after having an epiphany in the middle of a marvellously unnecessary lecture, decided to move his backside, and do something unboring for a change . Originally intended as a platform for the misguided and often misunderstood engineer to get some jazz, ||Shabd|| has evolved over time and now caters to a vast majority of engineers, who actually believe fun is an essential ingredient in a healthy human diet. The Editorial Board takes pride in hosting ||Shabd|| with scores of volunteers, to bring to the table everything from debate to creative writing to mad ads, in both English and Kannada. A two-day celebration of art and literature, ||Shabd|| has something for everyone. Hop on, then!
