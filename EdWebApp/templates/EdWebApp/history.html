The Editorial Board

OUR JOURNEY

Estd. 26 Oct 1985

1968

Way back in the late sixties, our college started bringing out the magazine under the name 'Jaya Prabha'. The board mainly consisted of professors and the working staff of the college. Along with a lot of compelling articles, the magazines also published academic papers highlighting the latest technology of that era in English and Kannada. The magazines contained a fair amount of advertisements which provided them with funds. The early editions had the journal reports of various non-curricular activities throughout the academic year and laid emphasis on students who excelled in sports, literary contests, debates, and other histrionic displays.

1970

By this time, the board had been infiltrated by students. The name of the magazine was changed to 'SJCE Magazine' and a third section dedicated to Hindi began during this era. Students had started expressing their opinions in the form of engaging stories, captivating poems and hilarious comics that featured the woes of their engineering life. The students began taking ownership of the magazine through their beautiful illustrations on the cover of the magazines. Photography and art found its place in the magazine during the seventies.

1990

It was during this time that the magazine gained its famed title of 'Jayzine'. The now colorful magazine was filled with pop culture references of the time. It gave refuge to people who wanted to express their mind and soul through words and art. Interesting collages showcased the 90's girl-power anthems and bromances. The magazines clearly showed us that the 90's had become the party central for youngsters and the college brimmed with the enthusiasm of the students in their roaring twenties.

2000

The magazine continued to be a platform where the students fervently submitted various artworks, articles, and poems. Around this time, multiple clubs had been newly established in the college such as Graycell ( quizzing club), SPICMACAY(musical club), and The Philosopher's Club. The introduction of beautifully illustrated bookmarks began in the year 2007. Keeping up with the ever-changing movie and literary culture, there were several pages dedicated to the current favorites of the generation. Multiple pages of the magazine were specifically designated to the artworks and photography submitted by the students which have been increasing vastly every year. The alumni of the college, who went on to become successful in their lives and respective fields, were interviewed on their opinions of their college life and the improvements that could be made academically.

2011

We see the magazine slowly evolve into what it has become today. The articles and poems sent in had increased drastically. A lot of beautiful photos, artwork, poems and articles were sent in. The magazine also had some color pages in which witty illustrations were made. To name a few, Sir Edit-A-Lot, Bamboozled, Opuners, and Polls have become a favorite among the students.
